# bro

Restate your last message. Drop the jargon. Say it the way one person explains
it to another.

- Keep the facts the user needs to act.
- Keep the numbers, paths, and commands exact.
- Drop the rest: no new findings, no new options, no new work.
- Short. If the original was one paragraph, this is two sentences.
